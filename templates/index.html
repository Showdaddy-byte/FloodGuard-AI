A real driving route (OSRM), checked point by point against live flood risk — terrain, water proximity, and current rainfall along the way. Not a "likely flooded roads" guess — every segment shown is backed by real elevation and water-proximity data.

Thinking of Renting or Buying Here?

A long-term flood vulnerability check for a specific address or neighborhood — based on elevation, distance to water, historical satellite water presence, and past community flood reports. This does not depend on today's weather, so it's useful to check any time of year, not just during a storm.
