🚗 Can I Travel There Safely?

A real driving route (OSRM), checked point by point against live flood risk — terrain, water proximity, and current rainfall at points along the way. It does not include tides, road closures, ice or snow, so always check local conditions and tide tables before travelling.

Thinking of Renting or Buying Here?

A long-term flood vulnerability check for a specific address or neighborhood — based on elevation, distance to water, historical satellite water presence, and past community flood reports. This does not depend on today's weather, so it's useful to check any time of year, not just during a storm.

    Get Alerted Before It Floods

    Save your home, office, or any location you care about. We'll email you the moment it reaches HIGH flood risk or above — you don't have to keep checking back.

    Phone/WhatsApp numbers are saved for when SMS and WhatsApp alerts launch — only email alerts are active right now. You can unsubscribe from any alert email at any time.

    Telegram alerts

    Get a Telegram message when the predicted flood-risk score for your community reaches the alert level, plus an urgent update if it rises sharply. Free, and you can stop any time.

    1. Tap Connect on Telegram and press Start in the chat that opens.
    2. Leave the neighbourhood blank to get alerts for the whole city; fill it in for just that neighbourhood. Add as many places as you like, in any country.
    3. In Telegram: /add Place, Country, /list, /pause, /stop to unsubscribe.

    Alerts show predicted flood risk from FloodGuard's model. For urgent help, contact your local emergency number.

    Flood Risk Dashboard

    Search a city to see live weather, animated conditions, forecast rainfall, flood score, and map risk area.

    Search a specific neighborhood for the most accurate result — flood risk can vary block to block within the same city, so "Lagos" alone is far less precise than "Lekki, Lagos" or "Maryland, Lagos".
